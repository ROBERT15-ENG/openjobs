1. Scope

This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information when you use {{APP_URL}} as a job seeker, employer, or visitor. It is designed to align with:

  • Australia — Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), including the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.
  • Kenya — Data Protection Act, 2019 and regulations issued by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

2. Personal information we collect

  • Account data — name, email, password hash, role (seeker/employer), sign-in method.
  • Profile & CV — resume text, skills, location, phone, work preferences, uploaded documents.
  • Applications — jobs applied to, cover notes, application status, ATS-related scores.
  • Employer data — company name, job listings, applicant pipeline data you access lawfully.
  • Verification (KYC) — where enabled, identity document metadata and uploads you provide.
  • Technical data — IP address, device/browser type, security logs, rate-limit counters.
  • Payment metadata — processed by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications — support messages and email delivery logs.

4. Automated processing & AI

We may use automated tools (including local or hosted language models such as Ollama) to extract skills from resumes and score job fit. Resume content is treated as untrusted user data; we apply technical controls to reduce manipulation (prompt-injection) risk. Automated scores are advisory — employers make hiring decisions.

You may contact us to request meaningful information about automated decisions that significantly affect you, consistent with applicable law in your country.

5. Who we share information with

  • Employers — when you apply or make your profile visible, relevant application data is shared.
  • Service providers (processors) — hosting, email (SMTP/SendGrid), payments (Stripe), optional Telegram notifications, and AI infrastructure where used.
  • Authorities — when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell personal information. Cross-border disclosure may occur (e.g. Australian users on infrastructure located outside Australia, or Kenyan users on regional cloud). We take reasonable steps to ensure overseas recipients handle information consistently with this policy and applicable law.

6. Data processing agreements

We use written or standard contractual terms with subprocessors where required. A current subprocessor list is available on request at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy version.

7. Data retention

  • Active accounts — retained while your account is open and as needed to provide the service.
  • Applications — retained for up to 24 months after last activity unless law requires longer or you delete your account sooner.
  • Marketing consent records — retained for 7 years as evidence of consent.
  • Security logs — typically 90 days, unless investigating an incident.
  • Deleted accounts — primary profile data removed within 30 days; backups may persist up to 90 days.

8. Your rights (Australia & Kenya)

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and associated data (subject to legal retention).
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with a regulator.

Australia: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) — oaic.gov.au.

Kenya: ODPC — odpc.go.ke.

In-app: Profile → Privacy & data (export or delete). API: GET /api/account/export, DELETE /api/account (authenticated).
