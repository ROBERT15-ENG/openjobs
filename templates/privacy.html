Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

OpenJobs collects account information (name, email, profile data) and application materials you submit when applying to jobs. Employers receive the information you choose to share in your application.

Account details, résumé uploads, job applications, saved jobs, and usage data needed to operate the platform.
