Frequently asked questions

How does visa sponsorship search work?
OpenJobs highlights roles that mention employer sponsorship or skilled visas (for example 482/TSS) and can match listings to Australia's skilled occupation lists. Premium adds alerts and early access — listings remain employer-declared, not a guarantee of nomination.
Is OpenJobs free for job seekers?
Browsing and applying are free. Optional Premium (including visa sponsorship alerts) is a paid upgrade shown in the job feed before you sign in.
How do employers post a job?
Use the employer dashboard to publish standard or premium listings. Premium posts get featured placement and outreach to matched candidates.
What cookies does the site use?
Essential cookies run the service. Analytics cookies load only if you accept them in the banner. See our Privacy Policy.
